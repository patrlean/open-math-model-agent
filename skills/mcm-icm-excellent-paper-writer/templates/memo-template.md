# Stakeholder Memo Template

```text
Date: [Date]
To: [Decision maker / organization]
From: Team #[Control Number]
Subject: [Action-oriented subject]
```

## Purpose

State the decision problem and why the recipient is the appropriate actor.

## Key Findings

Give 2–4 findings from the models, including the most important quantitative results.

## Recommended Actions

1. **Action 1:** actor, action, resources, expected effect.
2. **Action 2:** actor, action, resources, expected effect.
3. **Action 3:** actor, action, resources, expected effect.

## Risks and Contingencies

State the dominant uncertainty from sensitivity analysis and the corresponding fallback plan.

## Closing Request

Ask the recipient to approve, adopt, fund, coordinate, or implement the proposed plan.
